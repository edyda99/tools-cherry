Why the numbers move every year

The tax code adjusts the brackets and the standard deduction for inflation every year, using a price index the government publishes monthly. The IRS does the arithmetic and publishes the result in a Revenue Procedure each autumn. The rounding rule is why the new figures are round numbers:
