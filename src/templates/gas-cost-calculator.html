How to use the gas cost calculator

Type in three numbers and the cost appears as you type. There is no button to press, and nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Trip distance. How far you are driving, in miles or kilometres. For a drive there and back, enter the one-way distance and tick Round trip, which doubles it.

Fuel economy. How far your car goes on a set amount of fuel. In the US that is miles per gallon (MPG). A new car shows its EPA rating on the window sticker, and you can look up a car's rating on fueleconomy.gov. Your own average from past fill-ups is usually closer to real life. If you think in litres per 100 km or km per litre, pick that unit and the calculator converts it for you.

Gas price. What you pay, per gallon or per litre. Prices change week to week and town to town, so the box starts with an example: type your local price. The U.S. Energy Information Administration publishes national and regional average gas prices every week.

Split between people. Carpooling? Tick Split the cost and enter how many people are chipping in to see each person's share.

Price of a fill-up. Type a number of gallons in the fill-up box to see what that much gas costs at your price.

Everything runs in your browser. The numbers you type are never uploaded.

Common gas cost questions

How do I calculate the gas cost of a trip? Divide the miles by your car's MPG to get the gallons you need, then multiply the gallons by the price per gallon. For example, {{GAS_EX1_DISTANCE}} miles at {{GAS_EX1_MPG}} MPG is {{GAS_EX1_GALLONS}} gallons, and at {{GAS_EX1_PRICE}} a gallon that is {{GAS_EX1_COST}}. For a round trip, double the miles first.

How much is {{GAS_FILL_GALLONS}} gallons of gas? Multiply {{GAS_FILL_GALLONS}} by the price per gallon. At {{GAS_FILL_PRICE_1}} a gallon, {{GAS_FILL_GALLONS}} gallons costs {{GAS_FILL_COST_1}}. At {{GAS_FILL_PRICE_2}} it costs {{GAS_FILL_COST_2}}, and at {{GAS_FILL_PRICE_3}} it costs {{GAS_FILL_COST_3}}. The fill-up box on this page works it out for any amount at your price.

How much does it cost to drive 100 miles? Divide 100 by your MPG to get the gallons, then multiply by the gas price. At {{GAS_100_MPG}} MPG and {{GAS_100_PRICE}} a gallon, 100 miles takes {{GAS_100_GALLONS}} gallons and costs {{GAS_100_COST}}. The table on this page shows other combinations.

How do I convert L/100km to MPG? Divide 235.21 by the L/100km figure to get US miles per gallon. For example, {{GAS_L100_EXAMPLE}} L/100km is about {{GAS_L100_MPG}} MPG. You can also type L/100km or km/L straight into this calculator, or use the fuel economy calculator to convert between every unit.

What MPG should I use for my car? Your own average from past fill-ups is the most realistic figure. Otherwise use the EPA rating from the window sticker or fueleconomy.gov: the highway figure for a long highway drive, the combined figure for mixed driving. Higher speeds, hard acceleration, cold weather, short trips and heavy loads all lower real MPG.

This is a fuel-only estimate. It does not include tolls, parking or wear and tear. An all-electric car runs on electricity rather than gallons, so these figures do not apply to one.
