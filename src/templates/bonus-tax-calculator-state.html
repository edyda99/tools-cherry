What kind of extra pay is this?

Only California asks this

California is the one state that holds back a higher rate on bonuses and stock options than on every other kind of extra pay. Every other state uses a single rate for all of them, so this question makes no difference anywhere else.

Married filing jointly only, leave it blank otherwise. Leave it empty and we treat the pay you already entered as everything the household earns.

Why one return for two people needs both incomes

When you and your husband or wife send in one return together, both incomes are taxed as one, so the rate your bonus really lands in depends on the pair of you and not on your own pay alone. Social Security and Medicare are still worked out on your own pay whatever you put here, because those are charged per person. If you enter less than the pay you already gave us, we use that pay instead, since a household cannot earn less than one of the people in it.
