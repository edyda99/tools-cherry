The data file

Download the data (CSV). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers and holds every number behind all three charts, one number per row, plus the federal tax inputs (standard deduction and the income level where each tax rate starts).

  • chart says which chart the row belongs to, and figure_status says whether the number is OFFICIAL, an ESTIMATE, PROJECTED, or computed under 2026 rules.
  • input_amount_usd is the starting amount (a monthly Social Security payment, annual wages or an annual salary), and input_basis says which.
  • measure names the result and value_usd gives it, in dollars. cola_percent is filled in on the COLA rows.
  • source names where each number comes from. The last line of the file is the citation; delete it if you load the file into analysis software.

The missing month: October 2025

The federal tax brackets for 2027 are adjusted for inflation using the average of a government price index (the chained consumer price index, C-CPI-U) over the 12 months that end in August 2026. One of those 12 months does not exist. During the federal government shutdown in the fall of 2025, the Bureau of Labor Statistics did not collect prices for October 2025, so no October value was ever published, and it will not be.

{{GAP_STATUS}}

The Social Security COLA is not affected. It uses a different index (CPI-W) for July, August and September only, and every one of those months was collected.
