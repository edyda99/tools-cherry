What "I'm in the 22% bracket" actually means

It means your next dollar is taxed at 22%. It does not mean 22% of everything you earn. The first slice of your taxable income is taxed at 10%, the next slice at 12%, and so on — you only pay the higher rate on the part of your income that reaches into that band. That is why a raise can never leave you worse off, and why the share of your income you actually hand over is always lower than your bracket.

What about 2027?

The 2027 figures do not exist yet. The IRS publishes them in a Revenue Procedure in late October 2026, after the government publishes the last of the price-index months the adjustment is computed from. If you want to see how that calculation works and how much of it is actually knowable today, we keep a page that shows its inputs rather than just a table: projected 2027 tax brackets, and what is still pending.
