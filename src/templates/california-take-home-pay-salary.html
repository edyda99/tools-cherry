How this figure was computed

Every number on this page is computed at build time by the same open paycheck engine behind the California paycheck calculator, from the {{TAX_YEAR}} tax data file in this repository. Nothing here is typed in by hand, and nothing is copied from another site's table. When California or the IRS publishes a new figure we edit that data file, and the next build recomputes this page from it.

Gross pay
{{SALARY}} a year, taken as an even salary across the year.
Federal income tax
{{TAX_YEAR}} brackets applied to gross pay less the {{FED_STD_DED}} single standard deduction (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32). No credits, no itemizing, no W-4 adjustments.
Social Security and Medicare
6.2% on wages up to the {{TAX_YEAR}} wage base of {{SS_WAGE_BASE}}, and 1.45% on all wages with no cap. {{ADDL_MEDICARE_SENTENCE}}
California income tax
California's own rate schedule applied after its {{CA_STD_DED}} single standard deduction. Personal exemption credits, dependent credits and itemized deductions are not modelled.
California SDI
State Disability Insurance, which also funds Paid Family Leave, at {{SDI_RATE}} of gross wages. SB 951 removed the taxable-wage ceiling from 1 January 2024, so this one is charged on the whole salary. It is withheld after tax, so it lowers take-home pay without lowering taxable income.
