Expedition Packing Checklist

Two lists govern every trip: what keeps you alive, and what keeps you pleasant to travel with.

Safety Essentials

  • Map and compass, carried where rain cannot reach them
  • Headlamp with a spare battery taped to its strap
  • First aid kit restocked after every single outing
  • Emergency bivvy weighing less than a sandwich

Camp Routine

  1. Pitch the shelter before admiring the view
  2. Filter water while daylight makes the source visible
  3. Cook away from the sleeping area
  4. Pack tomorrow's breakfast at tonight's dinner

Group Kit

  • Shared shelter components
    • Poles and pegs split by weight
    • Repair sleeve in the lid pocket
  • Shared cooking system
    • One stove per three people
    • Fuel counted in breakfasts, not grams
